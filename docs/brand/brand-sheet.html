Brand sheet · v1 · 20 October 2026

01 · Name

Mistake-proofing for data

From poka-yoke, the error-proofing method Shigeo Shingo formalised in the Toyota Production System. A warning poka-yoke alerts before the mistake completes; a control poka-yoke prevents it. Pokayoke does both for every record before it reaches the database.

02 · The mark

One closed form, two roles

A navy P and a coral Y, drawn as fluid strokes that lock together.

  1. 1
    The P's sweep is the data flowIt arcs around the whole mark like a protective hand. The stroke never fully closes, yet the eye reads a contained shape.
  2. 2
    The teardrop counter is one recordA single drop of data: the unit Pokayoke inspects.
  3. 3
    The Y is the checkpointIts fork is where each record is passed or held. Its stem anchors the mark like the guide pin of a physical poka-yoke.
  4. 4
    The P flows into the YData passes through the check without breaking its flow.
  5. 5
    Navy is the system, coral is attentionThe same rule governs the entire interface.
